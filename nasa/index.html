Mission brief

Explore the Moon and Mars

Explore two low-gravity levels. Collect NASA probe records on both worlds and learn what each spacecraft did in the glossary.

  1. Follow the beacon and use arrows or WASD to move. Jump with Space. The Moon gives you longer airtime than Mars.
  2. Move close to a probe or data beacon and press E. Violet relays collect orbital records from spacecraft moving overhead. Each new scan awards 100 points.
  3. Spend points on speed, a shield, or a temporary power boost.
  4. Leave the red circles before meteors hit. The alien drones are fictional. Use F to clear them when they get close.
